# Internal Procedure: Sanctions Screening

**Document type:** Internal legal memo  
**Applicable to:** BMW Group Procurement, Legal, and Compliance  
**Last reviewed:** 2024-Q3

---

## Purpose

This memo sets out BMW Group's procedure for screening business partners, suppliers, and transactions against applicable sanctions lists.

---

## Applicable Sanctions Regimes

BMW Group must screen against:

| Regime | Authority | List Name | Check Frequency |
|--------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| US OFAC | Office of Foreign Assets Control | Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List | Before onboarding; quarterly |
| EU Consolidated | European External Action Service | EU Consolidated Sanctions List | Before onboarding; monthly |
| UN Security Council | UN Sanctions Committee | UN Consolidated List | Before onboarding; quarterly |
| German AWV/AWG | Bundesamt für Wirtschaft (BAFA) | German export control list | As needed |
| UK HMT | HM Treasury | UK Financial Sanctions List | Before UK transactions |

---

## Screening Trigger Events

Mandatory screening required for:

1. **New supplier onboarding** — before first purchase order
2. **Contract renewal** — before extending agreements beyond 12 months
3. **M&A transactions** — as part of due diligence
4. **Change in counterparty ownership or management** — upon notification
5. **Transaction above EUR 10,000** with counterparties in high-risk jurisdictions
6. **Ad hoc** — upon request from Legal or Compliance

High-risk jurisdictions (current as of memo date): Russia, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Venezuela, Myanmar.

---

## Screening Process

### Step 1: Identify the Entity

Collect full legal name, registered name variations, country of registration, beneficial ownership information (UBO).

### Step 2: Screen Each List

**OFAC SDN:** ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/285 or use SDN search at sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov

**EU Consolidated:** eeas.europa.eu/eeas/consolidated-list-sanctions_en

**UN Security Council:** scsanctions.un.org/search/

### Step 3: Assess Results

- **NO MATCH:** Document the search (date, name searched, lists checked, result). Proceed.
- **POSSIBLE MATCH (name similarity):** Escalate to Legal for review within 24 hours. Do not proceed.
- **MATCH:** Immediately halt the transaction. Notify Legal and Compliance. Do not proceed without Legal clearance.

### Step 4: Document

For each screening, record:
- Date of screening
- Entity name(s) searched
- Lists checked
- Result (NO MATCH / POSSIBLE MATCH / MATCH)
- Reviewer name
- Escalation (if any)

Retain records for 7 years.

---

## Escalation Matrix

| Finding | Action | Timeline |
|---------|--------|----------|
| NO MATCH | Proceed; file documentation | Immediate |
| POSSIBLE MATCH | Escalate to Legal | Within 24 hours |
| MATCH | Halt; notify Compliance | Immediate |
| Unclear ownership | Request UBO documentation | Within 5 business days |

---

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

Sanctions violations can result in:
- **Criminal penalties** for individuals (imprisonment in US, Germany)
- **Civil penalties** up to USD 1.3M per violation (OFAC)
- **Debarment** from US government contracts
- **Reputational harm** and press coverage
