# LkSG (Lieferkettensorgfaltspflichtengesetz) Due Diligence Checklist

**Applicable to:** BMW Group and direct suppliers (Tier 1) as of 1 January 2023 (expanded to mid-size companies 1 January 2024)  
**Legal basis:** Lieferkettensorgfaltspflichtengesetz (LkSG) of 16 July 2021 (BGBl. I S. 2959)  
**Official text:** gesetze-im-internet.de/lksg/  

---

## Scope

LkSG applies to companies with ≥1,000 employees in Germany (as of 2024). BMW Group must:
1. Conduct risk analysis in own operations and direct supply chain
2. Take preventive and remedial measures
3. Establish a complaints procedure
4. Prepare annual reports

---

## Checklist: Required Elements

### A. Risk Analysis (§ 5 LkSG)

- [ ] **A1.** Annual risk analysis covering own business operations
- [ ] **A2.** Annual risk analysis covering direct suppliers
- [ ] **A3.** Appropriate risk analysis for indirect suppliers if substantiated knowledge of violation
- [ ] **A4.** Documentation of methodology and results
- [ ] **A5.** Risk analysis covers all protected rights (§ 2 LkSG):
  - Child labor (minimum age, worst forms)
  - Forced labor / modern slavery
  - Disregard of freedom of association / collective bargaining
  - Unequal treatment of persons in the workplace
  - Withholding fair wages
  - Environmental degradation causing human health harm
  - Unlawful eviction / land seizure
  - Private security forces causing rights violations
  - Mercury use (Minamata Convention)
  - Persistent organic pollutants (Stockholm Convention)
  - Hazardous waste exports (Basel Convention)

### B. Preventive Measures (§ 6 LkSG)

- [ ] **B1.** Policy statement on human rights strategy (signed by management)
- [ ] **B2.** Supplier Code of Conduct in place and communicated
- [ ] **B3.** Contractual commitments from direct suppliers to comply with human rights obligations
- [ ] **B4.** Risk-based supplier training and capacity building
- [ ] **B5.** Audit rights and audit program for high-risk suppliers

### C. Remedial Measures (§ 7 LkSG)

- [ ] **C1.** Process for identifying and addressing actual violations
- [ ] **C2.** Corrective action plans documented and tracked
- [ ] **C3.** Immediate cessation or mitigation of violations

### D. Complaints Procedure (§ 8 LkSG)

- [ ] **D1.** External complaints mechanism established and publicly accessible
- [ ] **D2.** Anonymous reporting possible
- [ ] **D3.** Procedure for reviewing and responding to complaints
- [ ] **D4.** No retaliation against complainants

### E. Documentation and Annual Report (§§ 10, 12 LkSG)

- [ ] **E1.** Risk analyses and measures documented and retained for 7 years
- [ ] **E2.** Annual report published on company website (free access)
- [ ] **E3.** Annual report submitted to BAFA (Bundesamt für Wirtschaft und Ausfuhrkontrolle)
- [ ] **E4.** Report covers current fiscal year (due by 30 April following year)

## Supplier Assessment Questions

For each Tier 1 supplier, assess:

1. Does the supplier have a human rights policy?
2. Does the supplier have a code of conduct covering LkSG obligations?
3. Has the supplier conducted a risk analysis of its own supply chain?
4. Does the supplier have a complaints procedure?
5. Has the supplier been subject to any LkSG investigations or BAFA inquiries?
6. Does the supplier operate in high-risk countries or sectors?

**High-risk indicators:** Operations in countries with weak rule of law, child labor prevalence, conflict minerals, or restricted freedom of association.
