## Executive Conduct Frame

The BMW Group is fully committed to lawful and responsible conduct. Compliance is part of corporate culture and is fundamental to trust from customers, shareholders, business partners, and the public. Each associate is obliged to act responsibly and in compliance with the law.

The Code applies globally at all locations and for all associates of the BMW Group worldwide. It is intended to help associates identify compliance risks and avoid violations of law, statutes, and internal regulations. Compliance is an attitude for each and every associate.

## 1. Lawful And Responsible Conduct

Lawful and responsible conduct is integral to BMW Group and forms the basis for long-term success.

The BMW Group takes environmental and social responsibilities seriously. As a member of the United Nations Global Compact, it is committed to the Ten Principles and the International Bill of Human Rights. Based on its values and basic principles, BMW Group has created a corporate culture founded on trust, mutual appreciation, and tolerance.

This Code of Conduct formulates BMW Group's inherent commitment to respect applicable legislation and internal regulations issued to this end (compliance). It provides a binding framework for all BMW Group associates across business activities worldwide, at all locations in over 40 countries, in dealings with colleagues, customers, suppliers, business partners, public bodies, and with respect to applicable local and international provisions. Where additional business- or country-specific requirements apply, it can be supplemented by local regulations.

Associates must be familiar with legal obligations relevant to them, observe these personally, and strive for compliance in line with company values. This shapes BMW Group's public image and builds trust in its products and brands.

Associates are supported by a prevention-oriented compliance organization and a regulatory framework to ensure lawful conduct at all company levels. BMW Group has established structures, processes, and internal guidelines, especially for the regulatory areas covered by this Code. Essential regulations form part of the Group Compliance Management System because non-compliance can result in serious consequences, including fines, damages claims, and reputational risks.

## 2. Legal Framework - Overview

### 2.1 BMW Group Products, Services, Customers, And Markets

#### Product Compliance

BMW Group vehicles comply with legal product requirements worldwide. Customers can rely on BMW Group vehicles meeting all legal requirements, including type approval requirements, emissions regulations, and environmental laws. BMW Group monitors relevant legal information worldwide and integrates technical requirements into vehicle development. Product conformity forms the basis for developing and manufacturing BMW Group vehicles.

#### Product Safety

Product safety forms the basis of comprehensive product responsibility. Products in the Automotive and Motorcycles segments are developed and manufactured under strict quality management systems. BMW Group monitors products in the field and follows up on safety indications. When necessary, BMW Group notifies authorities without undue delay and undertakes required measures to ensure customer safety.

All associates must report observed product irregularities that could pose a safety or conformity risk.

#### Advertising, Consumer Protection, And Competition

BMW Group competes for customers with attractive products and services. Advertising must comply with transparency and accuracy requirements under consumer protection and competition law. Customers must receive information needed for careful and informed decisions.

#### Sales Organization

The quality of BMW Group's sales organization contributes to success. BMW Group uses a selective sales system. In the direct sales model, the local National Sales Company is the direct contractual partner of the retail customer for new vehicle sales.

Automotive sales and service structures are subject to specific legal requirements in almost all markets. Dealer, agency, and importer agreements define relationships with sales partners and protect competition. These laws specifically prohibit measures conducive to price fixing with dealers or encouraging price-fixing arrangements.

#### Tax And Customs

BMW Group's national and international operations have tax and customs implications. BMW Group respects applicable tax and customs legislation, reporting requirements, and publication requirements in countries where it operates. For intercompany transactions, BMW Group follows internationally recognized transfer-pricing guidelines. BMW Group rejects artificial tax structures and non-transparent jurisdictions without substance or economic value.

Associates help ensure tax and customs compliance as a core business activity through awareness, conduct, and active information exchange.

#### Export Control, Economic Sanctions, And Anti-Money Laundering

BMW Group complies with applicable requirements for export control, economic sanctions, and anti-money laundering across global business operations.

Foreign trade authorization requirements and guidelines must be observed when delivering goods, technology, or software and when providing services. Restrictions may apply to civilian goods if they can be used for military purposes as dual-use goods. Restrictions also apply to goods subject to restrictions in the country of final destination, including luxury goods embargoes.

BMW Group verifies customer identity and integrity under the know-your-customer principle, both to combat money laundering and terrorist financing and in BMW Group's own interests. Transactions with individuals, companies, or organizations on sanctions lists are prohibited.

#### Financial Services And Insurance

BMW Group Financial Services must build trust through tailored and customer-centric consulting. Statutory and regulatory requirements protect customers. The Financial Services Segment and its internal regulations are designed to ensure compliance. Implementation is continuously monitored internally and reviewed by external financial auditors and relevant regulatory bank and financial oversight authorities.

Customer data must be handled carefully and in compliance with statutory regulations and contractual arrangements, including data protection legislation and banking confidentiality rules.

BMW Group informs customers in clear and comprehensible language about its financial products and third-party products sold as agent, such as insurance policies. In many countries, this must be documented in detail. In some cases, BMW Group must inquire into customers' personal circumstances and prior experience with financial products.

### 2.2 BMW Group And Its Competitors

#### Fair Competition

Fair competition is a fundamental prerequisite for success through performance. BMW Group is committed to market economy principles and fair competition. Corporate objectives are pursued solely based on performance and in compliance with competition laws. BMW Group expects competitors and business partners to do the same.

#### Anti-Competitive Conduct

Anti-competitive conduct is not tolerated. BMW Group competes with other automotive manufacturers and providers in sales, financial services, vehicle development, and procurement. The most important antitrust rule is not to make market-related agreements with competitors, particularly regarding prices, bids, terms and conditions, production plans, sales quotas, market share, or human resources topics.

Deliberate coordination restricting fair competition is prohibited, whether based on agreement or informal discussions, including outside official events. Associates must avoid even the appearance of a violation.

Special care is required in association activities and industry meetings. These events must not be used to exchange confidential market or company information in order to influence market conditions. The same applies to market research and benchmarking projects.

#### Supplier Networks

When working with supplier networks, BMW Group ensures all parties' freedom to operate independently. Development and production involve complex division-of-labor processes requiring suppliers and development partners to work together. Network management must not illegally restrict parties' freedom to select business partners or determine terms and conditions.

#### Transactions And Merger Control

The purchase and sale of entities serves to strengthen BMW Group's competitive position. Transactions may be subject to merger control by antitrust authorities and regulatory requirements under foreign trade and payments legislation. Mergers and other transactions may not be completed until reported to and approved by relevant antitrust authorities where required.

#### Consequences

Non-compliance with competition and antitrust regulations may lead to fines, damages claims, reputational damage, exclusion from public procurement procedures, disciplinary action, and potentially criminal prosecution. These areas require detailed knowledge of case law and authority procedures. BMW Group Legal Affairs or BMW Group Compliance must be consulted in case of doubt and on legal matters relating to transaction application procedures.

### 2.3 Anti-Corruption

BMW Group takes resolute action to combat corruption. Corruption causes economic damage, affects vulnerable groups, and hinders fair competition based on performance. BMW Group takes a firm position against corruption and expects the same from business partners.

BMW Group's global production and sales network spans different legal systems and cultures, creating anti-corruption challenges.

Benefits for business partners and media representatives are only permitted within appropriate limits. Extra care is required for public officials and elected representatives. Officials, judges, politicians, representatives of public institutions, and members of parliament may not receive gifts, other benefits, or invitations that could appear to compromise impartiality. Such benefits are prohibited.

Facilitation payments are prohibited. These are payments made to a public official to encourage performance or acceleration of a required official process.

Compliant behavior requires every associate to separate private interests from company interests. Corruption frequently results from conflicts of interest where professional activities are affected by private interests. Associates must avoid situations that may result in conflicts of interest. Any conflict-of-interest risk should be disclosed and documented transparently.

Every contract should be awarded to the best bidder. Objective criteria must always form the basis for new business relationships. Business relations must be established or continued based on objective and transparent criteria. Commercial and personnel decisions, advice, or recommendations from BMW Group associates must not be influenced by private interests or relationships and must not be motivated by tangible or intangible advantages. Any appearance of impropriety must be avoided. As a premium supplier, BMW Group convinces business partners through products and services, not by offering undue advantage.

BMW Group associates must not accept, demand, offer, or grant any personal advantage in conjunction with activities performed on behalf of the company. Anti-corruption policies apply not only to direct financial gratuities but also to benefits such as invitations and gifts that could affect professional objectivity. Monetary and non-monetary benefits beyond customary and reasonable business practice must be refused. The same applies to benefits for associated persons, including family members and closely related persons.

BMW Group sets indicative value limits and defines criteria and standards of assessment to help staff evaluate whether benefits are customary and reasonable. Associates receive guidelines on documentation and approval requirements.

Transparent and reliable business relationships are key for anti-corruption. BMW Group expects business partners to prevent corruption and implements structured Compliance Due Diligence for selected business partners before entering a business relationship.

### 2.4 Data Privacy Protection

BMW Group strictly complies with data privacy protection regulations. Innovative information technologies raise questions about the right of individuals to determine how personal information is used. BMW Group considers this right highly important.

BMW Group takes data privacy protection into account when handling personal data of customers, associates, and business partners. BMW Group Data Officers support departments in this respect.

Personal details are recorded, processed, or used only if permitted by law or if the data subject has given permission. BMW Group is committed to sparing use of personal data and transparency in data processing. This ensures a consistent and appropriate level of data privacy protection throughout BMW Group.

### 2.5 Human Rights Compliance

BMW Group recognizes its responsibility as a company to ensure respect for human rights. This principle is integral to corporate culture and shared values. It applies to BMW Group's own business activities and to upstream and downstream global supply chains.

BMW Group helps ensure fundamental rights are observed through clear responsibilities and a wide range of measures.

BMW Group has signed the Joint Declaration on Human Rights and Working Conditions with national and international employee representatives. Further guidance is provided by the Policy Statement on Respect for Human Rights and Corresponding Environmental Standards, the BMW Group Code on Human Rights and Working Conditions, and supplementary internal regulations. These help BMW Group comply with statutory provisions for human rights and environmental due diligence and implement International Labor Organization Core Labor Standards.

### 2.6 Mutual Appreciation And Non-Discrimination

The wide range of perspectives and talents of BMW Group associates drives performance and innovation. BMW Group has a committed and competent workforce. Every workforce member is respected as an individual. Work with one another is characterized by appreciation, mutual understanding, openness, and fairness.

Discrimination and harassment are not tolerated. No one shall be disadvantaged, favored, or harassed because of ethnic origin, skin color, nationality, gender, religion or beliefs, disability, age, veteran status, sexual orientation, or other characteristics protected by law. BMW Group supports government policies designed to overcome effects of past discriminatory practices.

All forms of workplace harassment, including sexual harassment, are strictly prohibited. Each individual has the right to be protected from harassment, regardless of whether the alleged harasser believes the behavior is acceptable or whether the alleged victim could avoid the harassment.

Every manager must set an example through their behavior and ensure that their working environment is respectful and free from discrimination and harassment.

### 2.7 Occupational Health And Safety

Occupational health and safety management are top priorities at BMW Group. Worker protection and occupational health and safety provisions must be strictly observed to prevent health hazards. Safety at work is a primary duty of every individual. Managers are important role models.

BMW Group plans and operates facilities in strict compliance with safety regulations. This reduces accident risk and ensures smooth production processes. Managers must fulfill workplace safety responsibilities and corporate duties, ensuring associates who work in a facility are carefully selected and properly instructed.

### 2.8 Environmental Protection

BMW Group takes responsibility for the environment. Long-term success requires sustainable business practices. Environmental law and animal welfare regulations provide binding standards for BMW Group and are also expected of suppliers.

Associates responsible for facilities and activities that can affect the environment have special responsibility for compliance with environmental legislation. They are supported by the BMW Group Environmental Protection Officer and their network.

Environmental and animal welfare regulations, especially protection of soil, water, and air, prevention of unnecessary noise emissions, and prevention of excessive resource consumption, can affect all vehicle life stages. This starts with development, such as electromobility and safety of new and existing technologies, continues through selection, procurement, and testing of materials and resource-efficient production processes, and ends with environmentally compatible and responsible handling of waste and end-of-life vehicles.

### 2.9 Protection Of Company Assets

#### Innovation And Brands

Innovation, collective know-how, and experience are key to BMW Group's success. These innovations and capabilities must be safeguarded against imitation. BMW Group trademarks are among the most valuable in the world and must be protected.

In engineering and design, BMW Group uses legal options to protect innovations, primarily intellectual property rights including patents and design rights. Associates must inform the Intellectual Property main department about relevant innovations.

#### Confidential Information

Individual responsibility is required when handling confidential information. When working with suppliers, development partners, other vehicle manufacturers, the dealer network, or other business partners, BMW Group must protect confidential information, know-how, and business secrets.

Data and other information learned while working for the company may be used only as authorized. Before disclosing information inside or outside the company, each individual must ensure the recipient is entitled to receive it.

Depending on significance, additional safeguards may be required, including confidentiality agreements or audits. Observations or findings relating to possible negligent or intentional mishandling of confidential information must be reported.

#### Third-Party Intellectual Property

The confidential information and know-how of third parties must be respected and protected. BMW Group uses such knowledge only if obtained lawfully or from publicly accessible sources. Third-party intellectual property rights, including patents and design rights, must be respected.

#### Real Estate And Company Property

Every associate is responsible for protection and correct usage of BMW Group property and tangible assets. Real estate, equipment, vehicles, tools, spare parts, office supplies, documents, computers, and data media may only be used for company purposes. They must be protected against unauthorized access, loss, theft, damage, or misuse. Associates may not remove such items from company premises without permission.

Observations or findings relating to unauthorized access, possible theft, damage, or misuse of company property must be reported.

#### Information Technologies

Information technologies require special safety awareness. Electronic data processing is indispensable to operational work. Interference with systems could stop production and sales processes. Associates must adhere to security regulations issued by the relevant IT department and actively ensure compliance. Regulations cover development, purchase, operations, maintenance, and use of information technologies. Email attachments, applications, apps, internet downloads, and files from other sources must not be opened or installed until checked.

### 2.10 Transparency For Investors

Transparency builds confidence in capital markets. BMW Group relies on investor trust to finance global activities. This confidence is based on transparent financial reporting and equal treatment of investors.

External financial reporting duties related to BMW AG's stock-market listing and corporate bond issuance are wide-ranging. BMW Group provides clear and reliable information in annual reports and analyst conferences on capital-market-relevant matters. BMW Group discloses transactions involving BMW AG shares by persons with senior management responsibilities (Directors' Dealings).

Members of the Board of Management must ensure financial reports are accurate. These reports are based on information from all company divisions. Named associates must ensure contributions are accurate and complete.

Insider trading is prohibited. Insider knowledge imposes confidentiality and discretion obligations. Anyone with access to confidential information that could substantially influence the market price of BMW AG shares or bonds is an insider. Insider information must not be disclosed or made available to colleagues or third parties without authorization, except where colleagues require the information for a specific project. Insiders must not use knowledge directly or through third parties for their own trading purposes or to advise others to acquire or sell shares. Associates must not trade in relevant securities, such as BMW AG shares, while possessing insider information.

Corporate information that could affect market price must be published by the issuer without delay. BMW AG has established an ad hoc committee to review whether information could affect market price and advise the Board of Management on ad hoc disclosures. BMW Group associates must inform this committee via managers if there is any indication that subject matter could significantly affect the market price of BMW securities.

Transparency is essential when issuing new securities. Publications relating to new issues of shares and bonds must be accurate and complete. Prospectuses and company reports must contain all information needed to assess the issuer and securities.

### 2.11 Fair Treatment Of Contracting Partners

BMW Group's commercial success requires a fair balance of internal and external input.

BMW Group must respond flexibly to changes in the marketplace and customer expectations. The company works with contracting partners in different areas to secure required resources and expertise, with service contracts as an established and effective tool. Under this structure, the contracting partner performs a service for BMW Group autonomously on a performance-related basis.

### 2.12 Dealing With Authorities

Cooperation with authorities must be based on mutual trust and consideration, following relevant procedural requirements.

BMW Group strives to work with government bodies and other public authorities on the basis of full cooperation and transparency. BMW Group considers it important to adhere to legally established procedures for investigations and official activities. Exercising procedural rights is an integral and legitimate aspect of BMW Group's dealings with authorities. To ensure this and assist in discussions with regulatory and investigating authorities where appropriate, associates must confer with responsible advisory corporate functions according to the organizational structure.

## 3. Implementation Of The Code Of Conduct

Compliance with applicable laws is the personal responsibility of each individual associate.

All BMW Group associates must adhere to this Code of Conduct and adopt its principles as the binding standard for day-to-day work duties. The same applies to BMW Group's regulatory landscape, which clarifies applicable laws and provides practical instructions to avoid violations. Associates must actively inform themselves regularly about existing requirements and participate in available compliance training. Individual regulations are available to associates via the BMW Group intranet.

All managers must ensure this Code is observed in their area of responsibility. Managers must inform staff of the content and significance of the Code and sharpen awareness. Managers must do everything within their power to help staff act lawfully. Indications of legal violations must be rigorously investigated in consultation with responsible company departments. Managers must regularly and proactively verify legal compliance and communicate regularly with staff on this issue.

Managers promote a compliance culture based on trust, transparency, and appreciation. BMW Group managers serve as role models for values, integrity, and compliance and bear particular responsibility for avoiding personal conflicts of interest. They signal that compliance risks are taken seriously and relevant information is valuable in protecting the company. When risks are identified, managers initiate required changes transparently and prudently.

BMW Group's Compliance Management System creates the organizational framework for compliance with essential requirements. BMW Group has established a company-wide CMS to ensure compliance with main legal and internal company guidelines covered by this Code. The CMS's primary objective is to strengthen compliance and integrity culture and avoid company-related legal violations in defined subject areas.

BMW Group does not tolerate legal violations by associates. BMW Group expects all associates to comply with law, statutes, and internal regulations, even in case of potentially conflicting instructions from a supervisor or manager.

Culpable legal violations by associates may result in sanctions or termination of employment. If violations cause damage, associates may bear personal liability and be subject to penalties or fines imposed by courts or authorities.

BMW Group also offers anonymous and confidential reporting of possible non-compliance through the BMW Group SpeakUP Line. The same applies to risks and vulnerabilities that could lead to legal violations. BMW Group's ombudsperson and notifications@bmwgroup.com serve as additional reporting channels. Notifications are checked according to relevant regulations. BMW Group respects the prohibition of discrimination against bona fide reporting persons and is bound by the presumption of innocence.

The BMW Group SpeakUP Line is available in all countries where BMW Group employees work, in more than 70 languages, via local toll-free numbers. More detailed information on reporting channels is available on the BMW Group website.

Compliance with and proper implementation of this Code is monitored regularly across BMW Group. Corporate Audit monitors compliance and implementation, and risk-based control measures are implemented in particular by Corporate Security and Group Compliance.

This process may require on-site reviews of documents and IT systems, interviews with associates, and inspections. Associates are requested to actively support checks and control measures as required.

## 4. Interpretation

Compliance with applicable laws is firmly anchored in BMW Group corporate governance. Where reference is made to BMW Group or any BMW Group company, that reference implies compliance of such company with all laws applicable to it and nothing should be interpreted to the contrary.
