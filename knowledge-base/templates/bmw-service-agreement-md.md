# BMW Group — Managing Director / Board Member Service Agreement

**Template version:** 2024-Q2
**Governing law:** German law (BGB, GmbHG, AktG, ARUG II)
**Template type:** Service agreement for managing directors (GmbH) and, with adaptations, board members (AG)
**Drafting perspective:** Company-friendly
**Cross-reference:** `knowledge-base/playbook/negotiation-map.md` → Service Agreement row.

---

## Red lines (must not be conceded)

- For listed companies, the service agreement must be **consistent with the company's remuneration system** (§ 87a AktG).
- For board members of listed AGs, the **statutory D&O deductible** under § 93(2) AktG must be respected.
- Confidentiality clauses must **not cut across whistleblower protections** (HinSchG).
- Terminations should be documented **in writing**.

## Negotiable levers

Term, notice period, linkage between office and service agreement, garden leave, age limits, fixed and variable compensation, bonus mechanics, company car, insurance scope, side benefits, post-contractual non-compete, non-compete compensation, clawback, penalty provisions.

## Managing Director Service Agreement

Between **[Company] GmbH**, [address], represented by the shareholders' meeting, and **Mr./Ms. [Name]**, [address], the following service agreement is entered into.

### § 1 Appointment and Duties

Mr./Ms. **[Name]** has been appointed as managing director by shareholders' resolution dated **[date]**. The managing director shall manage the affairs of the Company in accordance with applicable law, the articles of association, any management rules, this service agreement, and the instructions of the shareholders' meeting, exercising the care of a prudent business manager. Transactions outside the ordinary course of business shall require prior approval by the shareholders' meeting.

### § 2 Term, Termination and Garden Leave

This service agreement shall commence on **[date]** and is entered into for an indefinite term. Either party may terminate it by giving **[e.g. six months']** notice to the end of a calendar month. Optionally, the agreement may provide that it ends automatically after a run-off period if the managing director is removed from office. The Company may place the managing director on garden leave, in particular in the event of removal or termination, while continuing to pay the contractually owed remuneration. **Any termination must be made in writing.**

### § 3 Remuneration

The managing director shall receive a fixed gross monthly salary of **EUR [●]**. In addition, variable remuneration may be agreed, for example in the form of a tantieme or a bonus-based compensation model, provided that the calculation basis is clearly defined. **For listed companies, the design of variable remuneration must be consistent with the company's remuneration system (§ 87a AktG).**

### § 4 Benefits, D&O and Company Car

The Company shall reimburse properly incurred business expenses in accordance with the agreed expense and travel rules. The Company shall maintain a **D&O insurance policy** for the benefit of the managing director on market-standard terms. If a company car is granted, the Company shall provide a vehicle of the agreed category, including for private use if so agreed; the provision may be revoked during garden leave. **For board members of listed companies, the statutory deductible under § 93(2) AktG must be taken into account.**

### § 5 Outside Activities, Confidentiality and Data Protection

Outside activities, board seats and other offices require the Company's prior consent unless already covered by the Company's interests. The managing director shall keep the Company's confidential information secret even after the end of the service relationship. **This confidentiality obligation shall not apply to the extent disclosure rights or disclosure obligations exist under applicable law (including HinSchG whistleblower protections), the information is already publicly known, or the clause would in effect amount to an impermissible post-contractual restraint of trade.** The Company shall provide separate data protection information to the managing director.

### § 6 Non-Compete and Post-Contractual Non-Compete

During the term of the service agreement, the managing director may not work for any competing business without the Company's consent. A post-contractual non-compete should only be included where there is a concrete need for protection. If such a non-compete is agreed, its **subject matter, territorial reach, duration and any compensation** for the restraint must be expressly regulated. In the case of board members, a clawback mechanism for variable remuneration may additionally be included.

### § 7 Final Provisions

There are no side agreements. Any amendments or supplements to this agreement shall require written form to the extent appropriate under mandatory law. If any provision is invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions shall remain unaffected.
