## 1. Parties

**Buyer:** Bayerische Motoren Werke Aktiengesellschaft ("BMW")  
**Supplier:** [SUPPLIER FULL NAME] ("[SUPPLIER SHORT NAME]")

---

## 2. Supply Obligation

Supplier shall supply goods ("Goods") as specified in purchase orders issued by BMW. Each purchase order accepted by Supplier constitutes a binding contract on the terms of this Agreement.

BMW standard: Supplier must confirm or reject purchase orders within 5 business days. Silence constitutes acceptance.

---

## 3. Quality and Specifications

3.1 Goods shall conform to all specifications, drawings, and samples approved by BMW.  
3.2 Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to IATF 16949 (or equivalent approved by BMW).  
3.3 BMW reserves the right to audit Supplier's quality systems upon 5 business days' notice.

---

## 4. Delivery

4.1 Delivery shall be DAP BMW's designated facility (Incoterms® 2020) unless otherwise specified.  
4.2 Time of delivery is of the essence.  
4.3 If Supplier anticipates delay, it shall notify BMW immediately and propose a recovery plan.  
4.4 BMW may, without prejudice to other remedies, obtain replacement goods from alternative sources if Supplier fails to deliver on time, and charge Supplier the cost differential.

---

## 5. Price and Payment

5.1 Prices are fixed as set out in each purchase order and are not subject to escalation without BMW's prior written consent.  
5.2 BMW shall pay undisputed invoices within 60 days of receipt of conforming goods and correct invoice.  
5.3 BMW may set off any amounts owed by Supplier against amounts payable by BMW.

---

## 6. Warranty

6.1 Supplier warrants that Goods shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship for **36 months** from the date of delivery to BMW or its customer (whichever is later).  
6.2 In the event of a defect, BMW may at its option: (a) require repair; (b) require replacement; (c) reduce the price; or (d) rescind the relevant order and claim damages.  
6.3 Supplier shall bear all costs of recall or field actions attributable to Goods defects.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 All intellectual property developed by Supplier specifically for BMW under or in connection with this Agreement ("BMW-Specific IP") shall be owned exclusively by BMW from the moment of creation.  
7.2 Supplier hereby assigns all BMW-Specific IP to BMW and shall execute any documents reasonably required to perfect such assignment.  
7.3 Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing IP. Supplier grants BMW a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use Supplier's pre-existing IP to the extent embedded in Goods.

---

## 8. Liability

8.1 Each party's aggregate liability to the other under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **the total purchase price paid by BMW in the 12 months preceding the relevant claim** ("Liability Cap").

8.2 The Liability Cap shall not apply to:  
(a) death or personal injury caused by negligence;  
(b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;  
(c) wilful misconduct or gross negligence;  
(d) Supplier's indemnification obligations under clause 9;  
(e) breach of confidentiality.

8.3 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages except in cases of wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

---

## 9. Indemnification

Supplier shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless BMW and its affiliates, officers, and employees from and against any third-party claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of or relating to:  
(a) Goods defects;  
(b) Supplier's breach of this Agreement;  
(c) Supplier's infringement of third-party intellectual property rights;  
(d) Supplier's violation of applicable law (including supply chain due diligence obligations under LkSG).

---

## 10. Supply Chain Compliance (LkSG)

10.1 Supplier shall comply with BMW's Supplier Code of Conduct (available at bmwgroup.com/suppliers).  
10.2 Supplier warrants that it has implemented risk management processes as required by the German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (Lieferkettensorgfaltspflichtengesetz, LkSG).  
10.3 Supplier shall cooperate with BMW's supply chain due diligence audits.  
10.4 Breach of this clause entitles BMW to terminate for cause under clause 12.2.

---

## 11. Confidentiality

The parties' obligations under BMW's NDA template (or equivalent executed NDA) apply to all information exchanged under this Agreement.

---

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for [1 / 3] years and automatically renew for successive 1-year periods unless either party gives 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.

12.2 Either party may terminate for cause immediately upon written notice if the other party:  
(a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 30 days of written notice;  
(b) becomes insolvent or subject to insolvency proceedings;  
(c) persistently fails to meet quality or delivery obligations.

12.3 BMW may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice. Supplier's sole remedy is payment for conforming Goods delivered before termination.

---

## 13. Force Majeure

13.1 Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform due to events beyond its reasonable control ("Force Majeure Events"), provided: (a) the event was not foreseeable at the time of contracting; (b) the affected party gives prompt notice; and (c) the affected party uses commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate.

13.2 Economic hardship, price increases, and foreseeable supply chain disruptions are not Force Majeure Events.

13.3 If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than 60 days, BMW may terminate the affected purchase orders without liability.

---

## 14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by German law, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG).  
14.2 Disputes shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Munich, Germany.  
14.3 BMW may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.
