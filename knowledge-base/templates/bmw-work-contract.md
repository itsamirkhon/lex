# BMW Group — Work Contract (Werkvertrag)

**Template version:** 2024-Q2
**Governing law:** German law (BGB §§ 631 et seq., ProdHaftG)
**Template type:** Work contract — services with a defined deliverable (events, build-outs, defined work products)
**Drafting perspective:** Customer-oriented (BMW as customer)
**Cross-reference:** `knowledge-base/playbook/negotiation-map.md` → Work Contract row.

---

## Red lines (must not be conceded)

- Liability clauses must preserve **intent, gross negligence, injury to life/body/health, fraud, guarantees, and mandatory product liability** (ProdHaftG).
- The statutory works defect framework remains the starting point.
- Any **shortening of limitation periods** must be drafted carefully and separately, and must not capture the carve-outs above.

## Negotiable levers

Scope of work, interface matrix, change request mechanics, subcontractor / direct commissioning structures, lump-sum pricing, payment terms, delay caps, shortened limitation periods, confidentiality, data protection, force majeure.

## Work Contract

Between **[Customer]**, [address], and **[Contractor]**, [address], the following work contract is entered into.

### § 1 Scope of Work and Schedules

The Contractor shall perform for the Customer the services specifically described in **Schedule 1**. The parties' responsibilities, interfaces and cost allocation shall be set out in more detail in **Schedule 2**. This contract consists of the main agreement and the referenced schedules; in the event of inconsistencies, the agreed order of precedence shall apply.

### § 2 Performance Conditions, Changes and Subcontractors

The Contractor shall perform the work independently and shall not be integrated into the Customer's organisation. The Customer may submit change requests, in which case the parties shall enter into constructive negotiations with a view to agreeing an appropriate amendment to the contract. The Contractor may use suitable subcontractors or, where agreed, trigger direct commissioning structures for the Customer.

### § 3 Cooperation Duties and Remuneration

The Customer shall provide the Contractor in due time with the information required for proper performance. The Contractor shall procure the equipment and operating resources needed for performance in its own name and at its own cost unless the contract provides otherwise. The Contractor shall receive the remuneration set out in **Schedule 3**. Unless otherwise agreed, the remuneration shall be structured as a lump-sum fee covering all services and ancillary obligations necessary for complete performance. Unless otherwise agreed, invoices shall become due for payment **14 days after receipt**.

### § 4 Liability, Defects and Insurance

Unless otherwise provided in the contract, the Contractor shall be liable in accordance with statutory law. In the case of slightly negligent breach of essential contractual obligations, liability may be limited to the typical and foreseeable damage; in the case of slightly negligent breach of non-essential obligations, liability may be excluded. **Liability for intent, gross negligence, injury to life, body or health, fraud, expressly assumed guarantees and mandatory statutory liability shall remain unaffected.** Claims for defects may, to the extent legally permissible, be made subject to a shortened limitation period of one year. The Contractor shall maintain adequate public liability insurance for the work performed under this contract.

### § 5 Force Majeure

If a party is unable to perform its obligations due to force majeure, it shall be released from the affected obligations for the duration and to the extent of the impediment. The occurrence of such an event must be notified to the other party without undue delay. The parties shall then promptly coordinate regarding duration, impact and any substitute measures.

### § 6 Confidentiality and Data Protection

Both parties shall treat as confidential all confidential information made available in connection with the contractual relationship and shall use it solely for the performance of the contract. The confidentiality obligation shall not apply to information already known or publicly available, information lawfully received from third parties, or information that must be disclosed under mandatory law. Upon termination, confidential information shall be returned or deleted, and evidence of deletion shall be provided upon request. To the extent personal data is processed on behalf of a party, the parties shall enter into a **data processing agreement in accordance with Article 28 GDPR**.

### § 7 Final Provisions

The general terms and conditions of either party shall not become part of this contract. Amendments and supplements to this contract shall require the agreed form. This contract shall be governed by German law, and the courts of **[place]** shall have jurisdiction to the extent such jurisdiction clause is valid.
