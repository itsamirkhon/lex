# BMW Group — Commercial Lease Agreement (Standard)

**Template version:** 2024-Q2
**Governing law:** German law (BGB §§ 535–580a)
**Template type:** Commercial lease (office, logistics, production hall)
**Drafting perspective:** Landlord-friendly (flip when BMW is tenant — see playbook)
**Cross-reference:** `knowledge-base/playbook/negotiation-map.md` → Commercial Lease Agreement row.

---

## Red lines (must not be conceded)

- Liability caps must carve out **intent, gross negligence, injury to life/body/health, fraud, guarantees, and mandatory statutory liability**.
- Exclusion of strict liability for initial defects under **§ 536a(1) BGB** only works if the mandatory carve-outs above remain intact.
- **Indexation is regularly problematic if the term is shorter than 10 years** — use stepped rent for shorter terms.

## Negotiable levers

Permitted use, fixed term, options, rent level, rent-free periods, rent adjustment mechanism, security, subletting, exclusivity / competition protection, repair and cosmetic maintenance allocation, traffic safety duties, insurance structure, modernization toleration, written-form cure mechanics, liability on landlord change, hazardous materials indemnity.

## Commercial Lease Agreement

Between **[Landlord]**, [address], and **[Tenant]**, [address], the following commercial lease agreement is entered into.

### § 1 Leased Premises and Permitted Use

The Landlord leases to the Tenant the premises marked and described in **Schedule 1**, including the related ancillary areas and parking spaces identified there. The leased premises may be used solely for the specific commercial purpose defined in the agreement. Any change to the permitted use requires the Landlord's prior written consent.

### § 2 Term and Options

The lease shall commence upon handover of the leased premises on **[date]**. It is entered into for a fixed term of **[●] years**, and ordinary termination is excluded during the fixed term. Any extension options may only be exercised by the Tenant in due time and proper form. **Section 545 BGB shall be excluded.**

### § 3 Rent, Operating Costs and Indexation

The monthly rent shall be **EUR [●]** plus VAT, if applicable. In addition, the Tenant shall bear the agreed operating and ancillary costs in accordance with this agreement and its schedules. If rent adjustment by reference to the consumer price index is agreed, the rent shall adjust in line with the index development; **for shorter lease terms (< 10 years), a stepped rent or other adjustment mechanism is the better drafting choice.**

### § 4 Handover and Security

The Landlord shall hand over the leased premises in the condition described in the agreement and the schedules. Handover is conditional upon the Tenant having provided the agreed security in due time. The security shall amount to **[e.g. four months' rent]** and shall be provided in the agreed form, for example by way of an unlimited, directly enforceable bank guarantee.

### § 5 Maintenance, Repair and Cosmetic Repairs

The Landlord shall remain responsible for the roof and shell of the building as well as the structural and central building elements unless the agreement provides otherwise. The Tenant shall be responsible for routine cleaning, servicing, maintenance and repair inside the leased premises to the extent the relevant measures arise from the Tenant's use or sphere of risk. Cosmetic repairs within the leased premises shall be carried out properly and at the Tenant's cost as soon as and to the extent required by the degree of wear and tear.

### § 6 Alterations, Subletting and Traffic Safety

Structural alterations, fit-out works and signage require the Landlord's prior written consent. The Tenant shall bear the cost of its measures, procure any required permits, and be liable for damage resulting from them. Subletting or any other transfer of use to third parties requires the Landlord's prior consent. Traffic safety obligations within the areas used exclusively by the Tenant shall rest with the Tenant, who shall indemnify the Landlord against third-party claims arising from any breach of those duties.

### § 7 Liability, End of Term and Form Requirements

The Landlord's strict liability for initial defects pursuant to **§ 536a(1) BGB** shall be excluded to the extent permitted by law, **provided that fraud and guarantees remain carved out**. In all other respects, the Landlord shall remain fully liable for **intent, gross negligence, and injury to life, body or health**; in the case of slightly negligent breach of essential obligations, liability may be limited to foreseeable and typical damage. Upon termination of the lease, the Tenant shall vacate and return the leased premises in the condition contractually owed. Amendments and supplements to the lease must be documented in a manner that preserves any applicable written-form requirements.
