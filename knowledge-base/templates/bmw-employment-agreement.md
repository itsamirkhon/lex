## Red lines (must not be conceded)

- Fixed-term arrangements and post-contractual non-competes require **written form**.
- Termination notices must be in **writing** to be valid.
- Limitation / forfeiture clauses must **exclude** claims based on intent, gross negligence, injury to life/body/health, and mandatory statutory claims.
- Probationary period **> 6 months** only supportable under a collective bargaining exception.

## Negotiable levers

Recognition of prior service, transfer rights, workplace model, working time, overtime regime, voluntary benefits, company car, garden leave, non-compete and penalty clause design.

## Employment Agreement

Between **[Employer]**, [address], represented by [●], and **[Employee]**, [address], the following employment agreement is entered into.

### § 1 Start Date, Role and Place of Work

The Employee shall be employed with effect from **[date]** as **[position]**. The Employee shall perform the duties associated with that position; a short job description should be included. The Employer may assign other equivalent duties to the Employee and, where agreed, may also determine another place of work, in each case taking the legitimate interests of both parties into account.

### § 2 Term and Probationary Period

The employment relationship shall commence on **[date]** and is entered into for an indefinite term. The first **[up to six] months** shall constitute a probationary period. During the probationary period, either party may terminate the employment relationship on the shortened notice period provided by law or contract.

### § 3 Working Time and Overtime

The regular weekly working time shall be **[●] hours**. The beginning, end, distribution of working time and breaks shall be governed by **[specific arrangement / works agreement / collective bargaining agreement / management instruction rights]**. Overtime may only be required to the extent permitted by law and where operationally necessary. To the extent overtime is intended to be covered by the remuneration, this must be stated transparently and in a numerically clear way.

### § 4 Remuneration

The Employee shall receive a gross monthly base salary of **EUR [●]**, payable at the end of each month to an account designated by the Employee. Variable compensation, bonuses or special benefits shall only be owed if expressly provided for in this agreement. Where special payments are granted on a voluntary basis, the agreement must make clear that no future entitlement is created.

### § 5 Vacation, Secondary Activities and Confidentiality

The Employee shall be entitled to **[●] working days** of paid annual vacation per calendar year. Any paid secondary activity requires the Employer's prior consent or must at least be notified in advance, depending on the chosen structure. The Employee shall keep the Employer's confidential information secret even after termination of the employment relationship, to the extent such confidentiality obligation does not have to yield to statutory exceptions, the absence of a legitimate confidentiality interest, or an otherwise impermissible restriction on the Employee's professional freedom.

### § 6 Termination and Limitation Periods

After expiry of the probationary period, the statutory notice periods shall apply unless a longer bilateral notice period is agreed. **Any termination must be made in writing to be valid.** Claims arising out of the employment relationship shall lapse unless asserted in text form within three months after they become due; if the other party rejects the claim or does not respond, the claim shall lapse unless brought before the court within a further three months. Such limitation provisions **must exclude** claims based on intent, gross negligence, injury to life, body or health, and mandatory statutory claims.

### § 7 Final Provisions

Any amendments or supplements to this agreement shall require **[text form / written form]** unless mandatory law requires a stricter form. If any provision of this agreement is invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions shall remain unaffected.
