**Template version:** 2024-Q2
**Governing law:** German law (BGB, GrEStG, GmbHG, UmwG)
**Template type:** Transaction-style purchase agreement — adaptable starting point for asset, real estate, or share deals
**Drafting perspective:** Seller-friendly / transaction-oriented
**Cross-reference:** `knowledge-base/playbook/negotiation-map.md` → Purchase Agreement row.

---

## Red lines (must not be conceded)

- **Fraud, intent, gross negligence, and injury to life/body/health** must not be contractually cut off.
- If warranties are given, the liability regime must clearly preserve mandatory carve-outs.
- In real estate transactions, **transfer mechanics, priority notice / registration logic (Auflassungsvormerkung), and any mandatory notarization (§ 311b BGB)** cannot be ignored.

## Negotiable levers

Scope of warranties, knowledge qualifiers, disclosure effect, de minimis thresholds, baskets, caps, limitation periods, cure rights, closing mechanics, conditions precedent, purchase price holdbacks, indemnities, MAC-style protection, confidentiality, allocation of taxes, costs, and transition risk.

## Purchase Agreement

Between **[Seller]**, [address], and **[Buyer]**, [address], the following purchase agreement is entered into.

### § 1 Sale Object and Scope of Sale

The Seller sells to the Buyer the **[property / shares / assets]** described in **Schedule 1** together with all associated rights, appurtenances and accessories included in the agreed transaction perimeter. Assets expressly listed in **Schedule 2** as excluded assets shall not form part of the sale.

### § 2 Purchase Price

The purchase price shall be **EUR [●]**. The parties may provide for a fixed purchase price, a purchase price adjustment mechanism, a holdback, or a combination of these elements depending on the transaction structure.

### § 3 Conditions Precedent and Closing

Closing shall take place only once the agreed conditions precedent have been satisfied or waived, including, where applicable, corporate approvals, regulatory approvals, waivers of pre-emption rights, delivery of release documents, registration preconditions, or financing arrangements. Closing shall occur on **[date]** or on the **[●]** business day after satisfaction or waiver of the last condition precedent.

### § 4 Transfer of Title, Possession, Risk and Benefits

Title to the sale object shall transfer upon **[closing / registration / full payment of the purchase price]**, depending on the transaction type. Possession, use, risk, burdens, and economic benefits shall transfer on the agreed transfer date. **For real estate transactions, the parties must provide for a priority notice (Auflassungsvormerkung) and the relevant land register applications**; for share transactions, transfer mechanics must be aligned with the agreed closing deliveries.

### § 5 Warranties and Liability

Except as expressly set out in this agreement, the sale object is sold on an **"as is"** basis to the fullest extent permitted by law. The Seller gives only those warranties expressly set out in **Schedule [●]**. In the event of a breach of warranty, the Buyer may first require cure or remedial action within **[●]** business days; if cure is not possible or does not occur in time, the Buyer may claim damages in accordance with the agreed liability regime. **Liability for fraud, intent, gross negligence, injury to life, body or health, and any expressly assumed guarantees shall remain unaffected.** Liability for ordinary negligence may be limited to foreseeable and typical damage. The parties may further regulate disclosure effects, knowledge qualifiers, de minimis thresholds, baskets, caps and limitation periods.

### § 6 Confidentiality, Notices and Publicity

The parties shall keep the existence and contents of this agreement confidential, except where disclosure is required by law, to advisers under confidentiality obligations, or to financing parties and authorities to the extent necessary. Press releases and public statements relating to the transaction shall require prior coordination between the parties.

### § 7 Final Provisions

This agreement contains the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the transaction. Amendments and supplements shall require the contractually agreed form and, **where required by mandatory law, notarisation (§ 311b BGB for real estate, § 15 GmbHG for GmbH shares)**. Costs, transfer taxes and transaction expenses shall be allocated as expressly set out in this agreement. If any provision is invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions shall remain unaffected.
